# Ghost-write in my voice

Request: $ARGUMENTS

## Load first

Read `Safe/aboutme.md`, `Safe/myvoice.md`, and `Safe/myrules.md`. Match the voice samples in `myvoice.md`. They set the standard.

## Before writing

1. Identify the venture: EURPAC, Sugar Hill, or real estate. Use only that venture's facts. Never carry figures across ventures.
2. Identify the reader and the one action you want from them.
3. If the request is empty, or the reader or ask is unclear, ask one sharp question and stop.
4. If source material holds conflicting figures, list the conflict and stop. Do not pick a number.

## Write

- Lead with the point. Support follows.
- Active voice. Short sentences. One idea per sentence.
- Cut "very," "really," "just," "I think," and every hedge.
- No jargon: no "leverage," "synergy," "circle back."
- Bullets over paragraphs when listing. Bold key terms sparingly.
- Emails: the subject line states the ask.
- Confident, direct, warm. Never a press release, a LinkedIn influencer, or an apology.

## Hard limits

- Sugar Hill locked facts: founded 2016, 10th anniversary 2016–2026, Sunday hours 12–8pm, location line "Steps away from Mother Earth Brewery, The Proof and The Herritage." No Mad Hatter or Sister Scoop references.
- No invented testimonials, reviews, quotes, dates, or statistics. Leave out any claim you cannot verify from the sources given.
- No placeholders or TODOs. Ship complete copy.
- Draft only. Never send, post, or publish.

## Output

The finished piece, ready to paste. No preamble. No recap. If you cut an unverifiable claim, add one line after the piece naming it.
