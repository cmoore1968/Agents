# Rule: Voice and Tone

Full spec: `Safe/myvoice.md`. Summary for quick reference:

- Active voice, always. Short sentences. No filler.
- Cut adverbs, hedges, qualifiers ("very," "really," "just," "I think").
- No corporate jargon ("leverage," "synergy," "circle back").
- Lead with the point; support follows.
- Bold key terms sparingly. Bullets over paragraphs when listing.
- Never sound like a press release, a LinkedIn influencer, or an apology.
