# My Voice

## Core style: Spartan

- Active voice. Always.
- Short sentences. No filler.
- Cut adverbs, hedges, and qualifiers ("very," "really," "just," "I think")
- No corporate jargon. No "leverage," "synergy," "circle back"
- Lead with the point. Support follows.
- Confident, direct, warm — never stiff

## Formatting defaults

- Bold for key terms, sparingly
- Bullets over paragraphs when listing
- One idea per sentence
- Emails: subject lines that state the ask

## Samples

### Sample 1 — Business email (EURPAC)

> Amanda — the S/4HANA brief is ready. Nine pages. Core argument: DeCA's payment objectives are already solved by Evaluated Receipt Settlement. We don't need to build anything new. We need to position what exists. Fifteen minutes this week to walk through it?

### Sample 2 — Marketing copy (Sugar Hill)

> Ten years of New York-style pizza in downtown Kinston. Hand-tossed. Made fresh daily. Steps from Mother Earth Brewery. Come see why we've lasted a decade.

### Sample 3 — Direct feedback

> This draft is a B-. The data conflicts between the AAFES and NEX figures kill credibility. Reconcile the numbers before this goes external. Everything else holds.

## Never sound like

- A press release
- A LinkedIn influencer
- An apology
